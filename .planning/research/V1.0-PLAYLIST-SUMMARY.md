# Project Research Summary: Playlist Playback

**Project:** BL-HAOS  
**Milestone:** v1.0 Playlist Playback & Management  
**Researched:** 2026-10-10  
**Confidence:** MEDIUM

## Key Findings

- Home Assistant's documented media-player pattern supports `BROWSE_MEDIA` and `async_browse_media` via `media_source.async_browse_media`; BL-HAOS already resolves media-source IDs in `async_play_media`.
- The ingress frontend currently has no HA media-source/auth channel. Custom playlist authoring therefore needs a secure HA-owned catalog interface, not a copied HA token.
- The bridge plays one validated URL at a time. Its process watcher marks playback idle on completion, so reliable sequential playback requires queue ownership, natural-completion detection, cancellation rules, and visible state.
- Persist ordered Home Assistant identifiers and resolve them at play time. Do not store resolved URLs, which may expire or contain access details.

## Roadmap Implications

1. Establish native HA media browsing/resolution and a secure catalog path for ingress; define persistence for saved provider and custom playlists.
2. Implement per-speaker sequential queue lifecycle, automatic advancement, current-item state, and failure/cancellation behavior across the integration and bridge.
3. Validate provider-specific playlist shapes and ensure the resolved stream is reachable from the add-on without leaking credentials.

These are sequencing recommendations, not a final phase plan. Phase grouping and optional controls should follow the confirmed requirements and plan-phase research.
