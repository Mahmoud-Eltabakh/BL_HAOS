# Plan 04-01: Aggressive Auto-Reconnect Engine with Exponential Backoff & Circuit Breaker

## Goal

Implement `AutoReconnectEngine` managing background connection health monitoring, exponential backoff with jitter, discovery fast-tracking for waking speakers, per-adapter connection locking, and circuit breaker protection.

## Must Haves

1. `AutoReconnectEngine` subscribing to `BluetoothManager` device discovery and property updates.
2. Exponential backoff calculation ($2.0 \times 2^n$ capped at 60s) with randomized jitter.
3. Fast-tracking reconnect attempts upon receiving device presence signals while in backoff.
4. `asyncio.Lock` per adapter to serialize D-Bus `Connect` calls and circuit breaker stopping storms after 5 consecutive failures.
5. Pytest test suite `tests/test_auto_reconnect.py` validating all states, timers, and locks.
